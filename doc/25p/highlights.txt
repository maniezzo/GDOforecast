This paper proposes BESO, a novel alternative to Distributionally Robust Optimization (DRO) that avoids predefined ambiguity sets by constructing data-driven scenario distributions. The approach extends Maximum Entropy Bootstrap, which captures complex empirical dynamics from short, non-stationary time series, by integrating an optimization feedback into the uncertainty modeling process. Compared to classical DRO methods, BESO is simpler, model-free, and more directly applicable in practice. Computational results demonstrate robust and effective performance across real and synthetic instances.
